Thanks to former Indonesian Vice-President Jusuf Kalla, or Juju, for making this site possible. Our Southeast Asian neighbours appreciate your reminder to be grateful for Indonesia's generous supply of clean air!

“For 11 months, they (our neighbours) enjoyed nice air from Indonesia and they never thanked us. They have suffered because of the haze for one month and they get upset.”
— Former Indonesian Vice-President Jusuf Kalla, or Juju

This site is a relaunch of thankyouindoforthecleanair.com, which launched in 2015 amid Juju's comments about the haze crisis in Singapore. Sadly, the original site went dark around 2019.

Indonesian Dania Rifki rebuilt this site in her spare time. Thank you for visiting!
